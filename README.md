# poke-hunter

## Legends Hunter Design Brief

### Core Concept
- **Genre:** Cooperative action-hunting RPG built in Roblox Studio.
- **Elevator Pitch:** A Monster Hunter–style experience where players travel back to an ancient era to hunt colossal, Pokémon-inspired beasts and craft gear from their spoils.
- **Narrative Hook:** Set in a distant past reminiscent of *Pokémon Legends: Arceus*, explorers venture across untamed regions to keep anomalous evolutions—feral predecessors to modern Pokémon—in check.

### World & Progression
- **Hub Village:** Central base with NPC mentors, crafting forges, a mission board, and lore archives chronicling discoveries.
- **Biomes:** Temple ruins, distortion-scarred wilderness, volcanic ridges, frozen fjords, and bioluminescent caverns—each housing apex prey and environmental hazards.
- **Hunter Ranks:** Players climb through rank tiers by completing hunt contracts, special trials, and investigative quests, unlocking more dangerous regions and monsters.

#### Top-Down World Map Concept
All biomes occupy a single contiguous island with the Hub Village acting as the connective tissue for every hunt. This layout clusters related regions while keeping traversal lines readable for open-world progression.


### Hunt Targets
- **Early Hunts:** Reimagined pseudo-legendary creatures (e.g., armored drakes, burrowing leviathans) that emphasize readable telegraphs and teach core mechanics.
  - **"Tempest Warden" (Dragonite analogue):** Patrols the Skyrift approach, using gust barriers and tail cyclones to teach aerial dodge timing.
  - **"Aegis Colossus" (Tyranitar analogue):** A living fortress in the temple ruins whose plated limbs break open weak points for heavy weapons.
  - **"Burrowjaw Leviathan" (Garchomp analogue):** Ambushes from distortion-torn dunes, drilling channels that reshape footing mid-fight.
  - **"Ember Cyclone" (Salamence analogue):** Strafes volcanic ridges with spiraling flame dives that reward coordinated part breaks.
  - **"Iron Atlas" (Metagross analogue):** Hangs from cavern ceilings with magnetized limbs, forcing squads to juggle vertical positioning.
  - **"Void Howler" (Hydreigon analogue):** Hovers above twilight marshes, warping projectiles that prepare hunters for reality-bending bosses.
  - **"Resonant Bulwark" (Kommo-o analogue):** Guards frozen fjord chokepoints with cascading scale shields that demand stun management.
  - **"Glacier Reaver" (Baxcalibur analogue):** Stalks permafrost valleys, layering ice walls that set up devastating shatter bursts.
- **Legendary Tier:** Climactic encounters against legendary analogues such as a distortion dragon inspired by Giratina, complete with multi-phase arenas and cinematic intros.
  - **Sanctuary Locales:** Each legendary roams an iconic biome that informs its tactics and loot, encouraging targeted prep work before the hunt.
    - **Rayquaza — Skyrift Pinnacle:** A chain of floating isles battered by jetstream gales and lightning storms that force aerial grapples and glider mastery.
    - **Giratina — Distortion Catacombs:** Warped caverns accessible only through hidden rifts that flicker across the frontier; approach one and Giratina tears fully into view amid gravity wells, phasing walls, and shadow geysers that echo its reality-bending strikes.
    - **Regigigas — Titan's Steppe:** Ancient terraces and stone monoliths that trigger seismic pulses, rewarding careful spacing during its colossal charges.
    - **Palkia & Dialga — Temporal Confluence:** Separate pocket dimensions connected by secret rifts; once hunters draw near, the twin blades manifest in mirrored arenas that lurch between tidal basins and crystalline clockwork as they bend time and space.
    - **Koraidon — Primal Cradle:** A lush mesa biome with sprint lanes, vine launchers, and territorial ambush predators stirred up by Koraidon's roars.
    - **Kyurem/Reshiram/Zekrom — Elemental Crucible:** A tri-spired caldera cycling between permafrost, magma vents, and storm fronts as the fused dragon swaps elemental rails.
- **Variant System:**
  - **Alpha Hunts:** Mid-tier escalations featuring expanded move sets, tougher break thresholds, and rare augment materials. Every creature in the biome roster supports an Alpha contract once hunters prove mastery over its base encounter.
  - **Shiny Hunts:** Prestige challenges with remixed arenas, exclusive cosmetics, and ultra-rare crafting cores.

- **Apex Wild Hunts:** Non-legendary anomalies seeded across the open world to densify the contract board while avoiding humanlike silhouettes. Their Alpha variants remix arenas with harder break thresholds once the base hunt is cleared.
  - **Temple Ruins & Subterranean Ramparts**
    - **"Sentinel Rampardos":** A bone-crushing guardian that chains headlong rushes with seismic tail spikes to train counterplay around narrow corridors.
    - **"Ironhide Bastiodon":** Braces behind rotating shield walls; crippling shoulder locks opens its core to sustained damage.
  - **Distortion Wilds & Rifted Badlands**
    - **"Riftborne Scolipede":** A colossal centipede that rides gravity shears for sideways charges, leaving venom pools that corral positioning.
    - **"Venomspire Dragapult":** Launches homing drakes through rift portals; disabling tail launchers reduces phase clutter in warped terrain.
  - **Volcanic Belt & Obsidian Heights**
    - **"Magma Arcanine":** A lava-maned predator whose rockfall howls and jetting flame dashes teach players to exploit thermal updrafts.
    - **"Cataclysm Camerupt":** Vents magma through dorsal craters in cascading eruptions, pushing parties to stagger pressure spikes before the arena floods.
    - **"Cindershroud Torkoal":** Vents pressure plumes along lava rivers, rewarding precise strikes on exhaust vents to prevent overheat bursts.
    - **"Pyroclast Turtonator":** Drops lava mines whenever its shell fractures, challenging teams to chain staggers before the arena erupts.
    - **"Forge Duraludon":** A siege tower quadruped channeling laser barrages through rotating dorsal arrays that must be vented via precision strikes.
    - **"Catacomb Steelix":** A magma-veined serpent that tunnels through basalt walls, demanding tremor reads before erupting in shrapnel geysers.
  - **Frozen Wilds & Glacier Lakes**
    - **"Glacier Frosmoth":** Blankets arenas in blizzards, then detonates climbable ice pillars, rewarding precise part breaks to halt freezes.
    - **"Avalanche Avalugg":** Carves skating lanes before collapsing them into ice tsunamis, making leg breaks vital to halt arena resets.
    - **"Frostmaw Walrein":** Chains permafrost waves with sonic roars, demanding coordinated staggers to halt blizzard crescendos.
    - **"Frostquake Mamoswine":** Chains snowquakes with tusk plows until leg breaks sap its momentum.
    - **"Rimeshell Lapras":** Freezes the waterline into jagged armor before shattering it into shrapnel barrages that demand shield timing.
  - **Luminous Depths & Sunken Ruins**
    - **"Crystal Noivern":** Uses sonic bursts to shatter glowing stalactites into cascading hazards while squads weave through vertical grapples.
    - **"Abyssal Dragalge":** Camouflages in brine clouds before unleashing corrosive sprays and tail lances from reef cover.
    - **"Tempest Eelektross":** A bioelectric eel that lashes between rift vents, laying charged nets that squads must sever in sequence.
  - **Skyrift Archipelago**
    - **"Tempest Staraptor":** Dive-bombs into cyclone drafts, reinforcing anti-air combos ahead of Rayquaza’s aerial gauntlet.
    - **"Gale Talonflame":** Dive-bombs between updraft columns, igniting thermal vortices hunters can redirect by staggering wing joints.
    - **"Cyclone Tropius":** A glider wyvern that seeds thermals with fruit burst bombs, encouraging aerial pursuit weapon mastery.
    - **"Skyrend Aerodactyl":** Pterosaur apex that dive-bombs through razor thermals, forcing well-timed grapples to ground it.
  - **Mesa Frontier & Ancient Grove**
    - **"Stampede Donphan":** Swings between armored roll-outs and tusk grapples, emphasizing spacing discipline along sprint lanes.
    - **"Quakehoof Mudsdale":** Compacts ground into seismic shocklines; crippling fetlocks slows its arena-wide quakes.
    - **"Thornbound Torterra":** Uproots root walls and moss turrets for terrain control; severing shell spires dismantles its bulwark formations.
    - **"Blightshell Forretress":** Anchors pollen mortars into terrain until shell seams crack, collapsing toxic spore barrages.
  - **Coastal Shoals & Stormbelt Cliffs**
    - **"Tidal Gyarados":** Whips up whirlpools and lightning-charged breaches during storms, acclimating teams to weather-driven phase shifts.
    - **"Seafang Carracosta":** A plated leviathan whose tidal shields deflect frontal assaults until shell crests are shattered.
    - **"Tidal Kingdra":** Alternates whirlpool sniping with tidal wave sweeps, encouraging split-role positioning and ranged pressure.
    - **"Maelstrom Sharpedo":** Torpedoes through tidal surges and lightning-charged breaches, teaching squads to read storm cues and reposition quickly.
    - **"Gloomtide Tentacruel":** Pulses bioluminescent traps under murky water; severing anchor tendrils halts tidal whip combos.
    - **"Thunderhead Luxray":** Floods the arena with ionized storm fronts; breaking dorsal fins interrupts chain-lightning pounces.
    - **"Stormcore Vikavolt":** Overcharges storm pylons as a living railgun; breaking capacitor horns prevents arena-wide lightning surges.
  - **Twilight Marshlands & Toxic Fens**
    - **"Bogshade Goodra":** Secretes corrosive mists and elastic tail lashes that test status cleansing and stagger windows in low-visibility arenas.
    - **"Bog Tyranitar":** A quadruped sludge colossus clad in barnacle armor that weaponizes swamp geysers unless its vent plates are broken.
    - **"Quakegorge Hippowdon":** Inhales swamps into sinkholes before erupting mud geysers; targeting jaw plating shortens the hazard cycle.
  - **Titan's Steppe & Quake Expanse**
    - **"Bedrock Gigalith":** Seeds crystal pylons that refract lasers across the field until hunters shatter its resonant cores.
    - **"Tremor Krookodile":** Burrows beneath quicksand ridges, forcing hunters to manage unstable footing and punish exposed forelimbs.
    - **"Razorcoil Sandaconda":** Knots itself into sandspout cyclones that collapse once hunters sever exposed tail segments.
    - **"Shardback Tyrantrum":** Headbutts crystal pillars into shrapnel squalls, rewarding precision breaks on its neck plating to blunt rampages.

### Combat & Mechanics
- **Action Focus:** Lock-on targeting, stamina-based dodging, weapon-specific combos, and part-breaking to disable monster abilities.
- **Weapon Roster:** Start with **six distinct archetypes** that cover slow power hitters (Greatblade), agile dual weapons (Twin Blades), technical counters (Gunlance), impact control (Warhammer), ranged pressure (Bowgun), and hybrid support (Glaive). Each archetype’s combo strings and signature attacks remain fixed regardless of the materials used; upgrades modify only the weapon’s passive traits and stat lines so balance stays focused on playstyle mastery instead of move list bloat. Legendary hunts anchor each weapon tree, ensuring that forging the final tier feels like conquering a mythic foe.
  - **Rayquaza Greatblade — “Skybreaker Crescent”:** A sweeping ultra greatsword inspired by Rayquaza’s serpentine form, emphasizing aerial launchers and wind-shear shockwaves.
  - **Giratina Glaive — “Abyssal Channeler”:** A stance-swapping polearm that bends space for support sigils or shadow lashes, mirroring Giratina’s distortion motifs.
  - **Regigigas Warhammer — “Colossus Engine”:** A momentum-driven maul that builds power as you land charged blows, evoking Regigigas’s continent-shifting strength.
  - **Palkia & Dialga Twin Blades — “Paradox Scales”:** Paired blades forged from time and space motifs, enabling stance combinations that rewind cooldowns or accelerate combo finishers.
  - **Koraidon Gunlance — “Primeval Runebreaker”:** A living relic weapon with roaring thruster bursts and counter-charges, channeling Koraidon's ancient combat style.
  - **Kyurem/Reshiram/Zekrom Bowgun — “Trinity Conduit”:** A modular bowgun that swaps elemental rails mid-hunt to unleash ice, flame, or bolt barrages, reflecting the fused draconic trio.
- **Forging Identity:** Monster-specific materials determine passive augments—bonus stamina regen, guard thresholds, status build-up, or team auras—and adjust raw attack/defense values, but they never unlock new attack animations. Hunters pick materials to tune stats while retaining the familiar move set of their chosen archetype.
- **Environmental Play:** Arena traps, destructible cover, geysers, and gliding currents reward tactical positioning over elemental matchups.
- **Multiplayer Scaling:** Four-player teams with readiness checks, revive tickets, and difficulty scaling based on party size.

### Loot & Crafting Loop
- **Monster Parts:** Hide, fang, core, and unique anomalies drop tables feed into weapon and armor trees.
- **Gear Identity:** Equipment mirrors the hunted creature's fighting style—defensive bulwarks, swift ambush gear, or status-heavy weapons.
- **Augments:** Alpha materials unlock defensive or utility upgrades, while Shiny trophies craft cosmetic transmogs and weapon skins.

### Live Ops & Replayability
- **Rotating Contracts:** Weekly investigations spotlight select Alpha/Shiny hunts with bonus rewards.
- **Community Hooks:** Achievements, hub trophies, and seasonal festivals keep the ancient frontier evolving.

This document serves as the foundational vision for **Legends Hunter**, guiding future prototyping, asset production, and gameplay iteration.
